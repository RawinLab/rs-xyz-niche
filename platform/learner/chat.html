I keep losing the room when I open with a number. How do I anchor without sounding aggressive?

2:04 PM
N

Good instinct to worry about tone — an anchor lands better when it's justified, not just stated. From your Module 3 reading, three moves keep an opening anchor credible:

  • Lead with the rationale, then the number. "Given the onboarding scope and the 12-month term, we're looking at ฿X."
  • Anchor at the edge of a defensible range, not past it — a wild anchor invites a wild counter.
  • Pause after the number. Silence signals confidence; back-pedalling signals the number was soft.
